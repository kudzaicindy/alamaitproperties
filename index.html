Who We Are
About
Alamait

Alamait Private Limited is a property management company operating in Zimbabwe and South Africa. We run residential, commercial, and student housing portfolios, and we stay close to the day-to-day details that protect cashflow and asset condition.

We are deliberately focused. We are not a conglomerate and we do not spread ourselves thin across unrelated services.

Our work is practical and measurable:

  • We keep vacancy time short through active marketing and fast turnaround.
  • We maintain properties early, before small issues become expensive repairs.
  • We give owners clear updates and keep records clean for accountability.
From the moment a landlord hands us the keys, we treat each property like it matters, because it does.

We handle tenant screening, lease administration, rent collection, maintenance coordination, owner reporting, and advertising content that promotes the properties we manage. The goal is simple: fill vacancies faster and keep good tenants in place.

Owners choose us because we do the basics consistently:

  • Fixed collection periods and disciplined follow-up.
  • Routine inspections and controlled maintenance.
  • Clear escalation when issues arise.
  • Fast response times and well-managed buildings for tenants.
